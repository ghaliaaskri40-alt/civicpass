INA § 316(a) • Continuous Residence

Have you taken any continuous trips outside the U.S. lasting 180 days (6 months) or longer?

Trips between 180 and 365 days trigger a rebuttable presumption of breaking residency unless disproven with lease/employment proof.

INA § 316(a) • Selective Service

Are you a male who lived in the U.S. between ages 18 and 26, and did you register for Selective Service?

Unexcused failure to register between 18 and 26 bars naturalization until reaching age 31 (or age 29 under marriage rule).

INA § 316 • Good Moral Character

Do you have any overdue federal, state, or local taxes, or fail to file returns?

If on an IRS installment agreement, bring official transcripts and proof of 6+ consecutive on-time payments to the interview.

Item 23-28 • Citations & Infractions

Have you had any traffic citations over $500, reckless driving, or arrests of any kind?

All citations must be disclosed on N-400 even if expunged or dismissed; bring certified court disposition records.
